**Title:** SatQuery AI — An Interactive Vision-Language Assistant for Multimodal Remote Sensing Image Analysis through Text Queries

---

## 🌟 Why This LARP Pitch Wins Every Hackathon Judge

Judges at Smart India Hackathon evaluate dozens of teams who read dry PowerPoint slides filled with bullet points, loss curves, and technical jargon. **They get bored.**

This **LARP (Live Action Role Play)** presentation flips the script completely. Instead of standing in a line like a classroom project, the 6 team members become **real-world stakeholders** who face life-and-death, multi-crore operational emergencies across India. 

Each member embodies a different user who has **zero technical GIS knowledge**, yet uses SatQuery AI to solve a critical national crisis in seconds simply by speaking or typing in plain English or Hindi.

> **The Golden Rule of this LARP**:  
> *"If a 12-year-old child or a non-technical senior bureaucrat listening to this doesn't get goosebumps and instantly understand what our AI does within 30 seconds, we rewrite it."*

## 🎭 Stage Choreography & Props (Zero Budget, Maximum Impact)

To make this look like a Broadway tech demonstration:

1. **Member 1 (NDRF Commander)**: Wears an orange emergency vest or yellow safety helmet. Holds a walkie-talkie or rugged tablet.
2. **Member 2 (District Collector)**: Holds a brown government file folder tagged *"DISTRICT RELIEF 2026"* and a pen. Speaks with calm administrative authority.
3. **Member 3 (Border Security Officer)**: Wears a tactical jacket or dark polo. Points with sharp, crisp military precision at the mountain radar screen.
4. **Member 4 (Municipal Commissioner)**: Holds a rolled-up blueprint or cadastral land map. Speaks like an outraged anti-corruption commissioner.
5. **Member 5 (Forest Warden)**: Wears a green/khaki cap or badge. Holds binoculars or a field GPS device. Speaks with passionate urgency about nature.
6. **Member 6 (ISRO Chief Director)**: Wears an ISRO lanyard or neat formal blazer. Speaks with visionary gravitas, tying all 5 stories into the technological crown jewel.
