## 🎯 Rehearsal & Judge Q&A Handling

### Q: *"How does SatQuery AI distinguish between genuine new buildings and temporary structures like tin sheds or wedding tents?"*
> **Answer:** *"Outstanding question! That is where our cross-sensor fusion shines. Optical images can be fooled by white tin roofs. But India's RISAT-1C SAR radar measures physical dielectric bounce! A genuine multi-story concrete and steel building exhibits strong 'dihedral corner reflector double-bounce' (typically σ° around -8.2 dB). A temporary canvas or thin tin sheet lacks structural mass and does not produce dihedral backscatter. SatQuery AI verifies structural permanence automatically!"*

### Q: *"Can this integrate into state government revenue and GIS portals?"*
> **Answer:** *"Directly out of the box! SatQuery AI exports standard WGS84 GeoJSON files formatted specifically for ISRO's Bhuvan Geoportal and state VEDAS cadastral servers. Any municipal GIS engineer can ingest our change layers with zero conversions."*
