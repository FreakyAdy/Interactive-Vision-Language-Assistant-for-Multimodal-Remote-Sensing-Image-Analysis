Operational Motivation

Why Single Optical Imagery Fails In Operational Reality

Operational remote-sensing questions cannot always be answered reliably using a single optical image.

☁️

Cloud & Shadow Blindness

Monsoon cloud cover conceals active disaster zones. Optical sensors cannot see through overcast skies, creating multi-day intelligence blackouts during peak floods.

📡

Structural Ambiguity

High-albedo soil, dry sand, and dense concrete produce identical optical brightness values. Disambiguating buildings requires SAR dihedral double-bounce scattering.

⏳

Temporal Dynamics

Single scenes cannot answer "What changed?" or "Has the built-up area increased?". Operational intelligence requires rigorous bi-temporal cross-referencing.

Explain why single images fail: cloud cover blocks optical passes, spectral brightness confuses soil and concrete, and change detection inherently requires bi-temporal pairs.
