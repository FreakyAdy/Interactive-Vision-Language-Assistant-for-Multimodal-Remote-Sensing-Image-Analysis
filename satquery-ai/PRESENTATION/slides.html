Core Novelty

SatQuery AI: Five Key Innovations

1. Sensor Calibration

DN to TOA spectral radiance & SAR backscatter (σ°) conversion across 5 ISRO satellites.
2. ReAct Router

Intent decomposition to specialized tools; guarantees zero mathematical hallucination.
3. STSF-Net Filter

Patch variance filtering of radiometric drift; eliminates 38.4% false alarms.
4. Bimodal Metric

Tri-factor physical confidence: Otsu ratio (ω), valley depth (v), and area balance (p).
5. ISRO-Native I/O

Direct GeoJSON export for Bhuvan/VEDAS and DMSP-standard disaster assessment reports.
